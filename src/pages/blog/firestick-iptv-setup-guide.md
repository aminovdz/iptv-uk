The Amazon Fire TV Stick is the most popular device for IPTV in the UK due to its low cost, remote control, and powerful streaming capabilities. However, because premium IPTV apps aren't always in the official Amazon App Store, you need to "sideload" them.

Don't worry—it's incredibly easy. Here is the step-by-step guide to setting up IPTV on your Firestick.

## Step 1: Enable 'Apps from Unknown Sources'
Before you can install third-party apps, you must tell your Firestick to allow it.
1. Go to the Firestick **Home** screen.
2. Navigate to **Settings** (the gear icon on the far right).
3. Click on **My Fire TV**.
4. Select **Developer Options**. *(If you don't see this, go to 'About', highlight your Fire TV Stick name, and click the center button on your remote 7 times to reveal it).*
5. Turn **ON** 'Apps from Unknown Sources'.

## Step 2: Install the Downloader App
The Downloader app allows you to download apps directly from the internet.
1. Go back to the **Home** screen and select the **Find** (magnifying glass) icon.
2. Search for **"Downloader"**.
3. Click on the orange Downloader icon and select **Download/Get**.
4. Once installed, **Open** the app and click **Allow** when prompted for storage permissions.

## Step 3: Download Your IPTV Player
We highly recommend **IPTV Smarters Pro** for beginners.
1. In the Downloader app, click the URL search bar.
2. Type in the Smarters Pro downloader code (you can easily find the official APK link online or ask your provider). 
3. Click **Go**.
4. The file will download. Once finished, click **Install**.
5. After installation, click **Done** and then **Delete** the APK file to save space on your Firestick.

## Step 4: Login to Your IPTV Service
1. Return to your Firestick Home screen, go to your apps, and open **IPTV Smarters Pro**.
2. Select **"Login with Xtream Codes API"** (this is the easiest method).
3. You will see four boxes:
   - **Any Name**: Type "iptvuk" (or anything you like).
   - **Username**: Enter the username provided in your welcome email.
   - **Password**: Enter the password provided in your welcome email.
   - **URL**: Enter the Portal URL provided by your service.
4. Click **Add User**.

## Step 5: Start Streaming!
Once logged in, the app will briefly download the Live TV channels, Movies (VOD), and Series lists. 
You are now ready to enjoy premium 4K streaming directly on your TV. 

**Pro Tip:** If you experience buffering, ensure your Firestick is plugged directly into the wall outlet for power (not the TV USB port) and try connecting via a 5GHz Wi-Fi network!
