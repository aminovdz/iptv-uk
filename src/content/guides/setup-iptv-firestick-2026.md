## Step 1: Enable Developer Options on Fire TV {#step-1-developer-options}

By default, Amazon hides Developer Options on newer Fire OS updates. Here is how to unlock it in 15 seconds:

1. Navigate to **Settings** (gear icon on the right side of the home screen).
2. Click **My Fire TV** &rarr; **About**.
3. Highlight your device name (e.g. *Fire TV Stick 4K*) and click the center remote button **7 times quickly**.
4. A notification will appear at the bottom: *"No need, you are already a developer."*
5. Press Back. You will now see **Developer Options** listed under *My Fire TV*.
6. Click **Developer Options** &rarr; **Install Unknown Apps** and turn it **ON** for the Downloader app (in the next step).

## Step 4: Connect Your British IPTV Subscription {#step-4-connect-credentials}

1. Launch your installed player (TiviMate or Smarters).
2. When prompted for playlist type, select **Xtream Codes API** (this is much faster and more reliable than M3U).
3. Fill in the three parameters received on your **WhatsApp activation message**:
   - **Server URL / Portal:** `http://line.britishiptv.pro:80` (or your custom secure server port)
   - **Username:** `your_username`
   - **Password:** `your_password`
4. Click **Next / Add Playlist**.
5. Your Firestick will automatically download the complete channel index, EPG schedule, and on-demand titles in under 10 seconds.

## Troubleshooting Common Firestick Issues

- **Black Screen on Channel Change:** Go to *Settings > Playback > Tuner* and toggle between Software and Hardware decoder.
- **Buffering at 3 PM:** Your UK ISP (BT/Virgin) might be throttling traffic. Enable your VPN or contact British IPTV support on WhatsApp for a port re-route.
- **Storage Low Warning:** In Fire TV *Settings > Applications > Manage Installed Applications*, clear cache on background apps.
