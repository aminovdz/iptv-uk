## The 3PM Blackout Rule Explained (Article 48) {#article-48-explained}

The **3 PM blackout rule** was introduced in the 1960s by former Burnley chairman Bob Lord. Lord argued that if top-flight football matches were broadcast on Saturday afternoons, spectators would stay home rather than buy tickets at local lower-league stadiums (Championship, League One, League Two, and non-league).

Under UEFA Article 48:
- No live football may be broadcast on UK domestic television between **2:45 PM and 5:15 PM** on any Saturday during the football season.
- As a result, domestic broadcasters only air approximately **200 of the 380 total Premier League matches** each season.
- Over **180 matches—nearly half the entire season—are completely blacked out** for viewers in the UK relying on traditional cable or satellite.
